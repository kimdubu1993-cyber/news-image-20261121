One front page for Saturday, 21 November 2026: Russia's overnight barrage of more than 400 drones and 110 missiles hit Kyiv and sent missiles falling into Poland, which Warsaw called a deliberate provocation; an AI stock sell-off dragged the S&P 500 into correction; the COP31 climate talks in Antalya deadlocked over fossil fuels and ran past their deadline; a magnitude 6.9 earthquake shook Peru's Andes and was felt in Lima; Mexico captured FBI most-wanted cartel boss "Chapo Isidro" in a joint raid with the US; a fire at the Hartha power plant near Basra killed at least four people; and a pancreatic-cancer pill, daraxonrasib, left tumours undetectable in one in twelve patients in a small trial. Each story is drawn as its own panel with a short headline and a simple illustration, under a masthead naming the day.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies the pre-rendered `news.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The program is standard-library only and fully deterministic: run it twice and the outputs are byte-identical. `draw.py` is the Pillow script that rendered `news.png`; it is build-time only and not needed at run time.
